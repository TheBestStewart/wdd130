BYU-Idaho Center

The BYU-Idaho Center is one of the largest indoor venues in Rexburg and a great option for indoor activities. It’s located on the campus of Brigham Young University–Idaho and hosts many events throughout the year.

FatCats

A popular entertainment center where you can bowl, watch movies, or play arcade games. It’s a great place for friends or groups to hang out indoors.

Gravity Factory

Gravity Factory is a large indoor trampoline and adventure park in Rexburg designed for all ages. It’s basically a mix of a trampoline park, obstacle course, and activity center all in one.

Rexburg Re-Escape

Rexburg Re-Escape is an indoor escape room experience where you and your group are “locked” in a themed room and have about 60 minutes to solve puzzles and escape.

Legacy Flight Museum

Legacy Flight Museum is a small but really cool aviation museum located right next to the Rexburg airport. It’s all about historic military aircraft and the people who flew them.

Heber Hatchets

Heber Hatchets Axe Throwing in Rexburg is a popular indoor recreation venue offering axe throwing for dates, groups, and parties, featuring coached sessions.
