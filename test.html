Teton Lakes Golf Course

This golf course features 27 holes in total, with 3 different 9-hole tracks. Expect to pay $14.50 for the 9-hole or $20.50 for the 18-hole, and bring your own clubs.

Nature Park

A good place to go to experience nature and escape the world for a while. Wonderful in all seasons.

Smith Park

There is lots of space here, and it's usually less busy than the other parks in the area.

BYU-Idaho Ropes Course

If you're not afraid of heights (or want to face that fear) the ropes course might be for you! It's priced at 10$ a person, with separate group pricing based on size.

Porter Park

This park has a playground, and if you go in the summer, there is a spray park. They also have Tennis Courts, rented at $10 per hour.

DonJo Sports Complex

If you want baseball fields DonJo is your place to go! There are also tennis courts nearby, and a football field.
