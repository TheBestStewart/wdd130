Client Report

How did you decide what content and images to use for your site plan?

After having the idea to make a website based on LEGOs I decided to have pages referencing my two favorites. The timeline idea was based on some feedback I received from other members of the class.

How did you communicate with your developer? Were there any issues with communication?

We used a combination of texting and Microsoft Teams to communicate. There weren't any issues communicating.

Did your developer meet your expectations for your site?

Partially, he didn't add the Junior Developer to the GitHub so the child pages were left unfinished.

What will you do differently next time you hire a team to do a project for you?

Make sure I research the team first to understand their skill level and commitment to projects.

Lead Developer Report

How did you manage your development team and communicate the tasks to your junior dev?

We used texting to communicate. I added my Junior Developer to my GitHub, gave him the site plan, and asked him to reach out if he had any questions.

How did you communicate with your client? Were there any issues?

We communicated over text. Descriptions from the client were a little vague, other than that no issues.

How did you handle publishing your client's site and sharing it with the client?

I used GitHub to publish the site. The client was able to use a link on the main GitHub page to view the site, and they were added as a contributer so they could see the code.

How was your experience with managing your site with GitHub?

It was great. I was able to easily publish my site using it.

General Experience and Reflections

What was the most challenging part of this project for you? Were you able to overcome it?

Communication. It was pretty difficult to get good communication between group members, though in the end it all worked out.

What did you enjoy most about this project?

I really enjoyed figuring out how to implement complicated functions to the child pages of my Junior Developer site.

What would you do differently if you could do the project again?

I would start communication earlier and try my best to get the project done quickly.

Do you feel more confident in working on real-world projects after this experience? Explain.

Yes. I was able to finish everything I needed to on time, and I learned a lot about HTML and CSS (and even a little Javascript) in the process.
