Local to Rexburg - Affordable Restaurants

Da Pineapple Grill

At Da Pineapple Grill, you can indulge in a diverse menu that blends the best of Hawaiian and Asian cuisines, perfect for satisfying your cravings without breaking the bank. With an emphasis on fresh ingredients and bold flavors, this spot is a hit among students looking for affordable yet delicious meal options. Whether you're in the mood for a hearty poke bowl or a plate of savory teriyaki chicken, Da Pineapple Grill has something to delight every palate. Plus, their generous portion sizes ensure you'll leave both your stomach and wallet happy.

Price: $10-$15

Da Pineapple Grill Menu

Millhollow Frozen Yogurt & Deli

Millhollow Frozen Yogurt & Deli is an excellent choice for college students on a budget who crave variety and quality. This cozy spot offers an array of delicious sandwiches and comforting soups, perfect for a hearty meal between classes. Don't forget to treat yourself to their renowned frozen yogurt, available in multiple flavors and toppings, all without putting a dent in your wallet. It’s the perfect blend of taste and affordability in a friendly, laid-back atmosphere.

Ice cream: $5-$7 (Good size portion)

Original Thai

When you're craving a break from the usual fare, Original Thai steps in with a vibrant array of Thai dishes that satisfy both your palate and your wallet. The menu boasts an impressive variety of curry, noodle, and rice dishes, each bursting with authentic flavors and fresh ingredients. Plus, with their budget-friendly prices, you can explore new culinary experiences without worrying about breaking the bank. Whether you're a fan of the spicy Pad Thai or the subtler Green Curry, there's something deliciously different for every adventurous eater.

Price: $15-$18 (Although more expensive, the food portions are well worth the price!)

Pizza Pie Cafe

Pizza Pie Cafe in Rexburg is a beloved spot among college students, thanks to its budget-friendly buffet that won't break the bank. Though it's a smaller chain with only a few locations, it maintains a cozy, home-town atmosphere. Here, you can indulge in an unlimited selection of pizzas, pastas, and salads, making it the perfect place for a filling meal without spending a fortune. Whether you're craving a classic pepperoni pizza, a hearty bowl of Alfredo pasta, or a fresh and crisp salad, Pizza Pie Cafe has got you covered. It's an ideal spot for study groups, casual hangouts, or just treating yourself after a long day of classes.

Price: $10 for full meal

Pizza Pie Cafe Menu

Casa De Ochoa

Casa De Ochoa stands out in Rexburg for its authentic Mexican cuisine, bringing the vibrant flavors of Mexico right to your plate without breaking the bank. Students love the combination of generous portion sizes and affordable prices, making it an ideal spot for a satisfying meal after a busy day of classes. Whether you're craving tacos, burritos, or enchiladas, Casa De Ochoa offers an extensive menu that promises a delicious dining experience. Don't miss out on their homemade salsas and fresh guacamole, which add the perfect touch to any dish!

Price: $10-$20 for Lunch and Dinner

The Hickory

The Hickory in Rexburg is well-known for its mouth-watering BBQ dishes and American comfort food, perfect for when you're craving a hearty meal without breaking the bank. From tender ribs and juicy pulled pork to classic mac and cheese, this spot delivers rich flavors that won't empty your wallet. It's a popular choice for students looking to enjoy a satisfying meal after a long day of classes or for a laid-back weekend treat. The casual atmosphere and generous portions make it an excellent place to relax and indulge.

Price: Select few sandwiches for $12-$20
Although more expensive, it makes up for it in the amount of food you get.
